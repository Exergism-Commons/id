Exergism Commons Identifiers

Exergism Commons · identifier authority

Persistent identifiers that outlive the hosting.

id.exergism.org is the high-stability semantic identifier surface for Exergism Commons. Repositories and websites remain authoritative for their own domains; this host provides persistent public identity and dereferencing.

Current publication state. Funding adopted the Exergism Commons identifier authority in its merged machine-readable governance layer on 2 September 2026. Governance's commons# architecture remains proposed in draft PR #1 and is not presented here as operative.

Migration / reserved surfaces

  • https://id.exergism.org/exergism# — reserved resolution surface; canonical Exergism migration remains project-controlled.
  • https://id.exergism.org/ecl# — reserved pending ECL's explicit release migration.
  • https://id.exergism.org/commons# and https://id.exergism.org/ontology/commons — proposed by Governance PR #1; intentionally not registered as an adopted semantic surface while that architecture remains draft/non-operative.
  • https://id.exergism.org/entity/... — reserved for a separately specified cross-project entity layer.

Where the authority lives

Funding semantics and governance records remain authoritative in Exergism-Commons/funding. The operational funding website is funding.exergism.org. This resolver must not manufacture policy, approval or legal authority merely because it can dereference an identifier.

Persistence contract

Allocation, immutability, redirects, authority boundaries and deprecation are defined in the repository's persistence policy.
