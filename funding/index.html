funding#

Human-readable documentation for https://id.exergism.org/funding#. Open a term IRI such as funding#InstitutionalFundingState to inspect the RDF facts published for that term.

Adopted namespace. Funding defines the semantics; id.exergism.org provides stable identity and resolution. The current ontology identifies itself as 0.2.0-pre1 (https://id.exergism.org/ontology/funding/0.2.0-pre1) and is published byte-for-byte from funding@00a11736…. Shared identity, provenance and institutional governance terms are reused from commons# and governance# rather than declared here.
