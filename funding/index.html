funding#

Human-readable documentation for https://id.exergism.org/funding#. This namespace contains funding-specific terms; shared identity/provenance terms come from commons# and institutional decision, voting and conflict terms come from governance#.

Adopted namespace. Funding defines funding-specific semantics; id.exergism.org provides stable identity and resolution. The current ontology identifies itself as 0.2.0-pre1.
